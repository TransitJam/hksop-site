About ZAP!

Thank you for using ZAP! to support a safer, more walkable Hong Kong, one data point at a time.

By pressing a button on your daily walk, you'll help transport planners isolate high-density blackspots, persistent road hazards, and dangerous obstacles that disrupt pedestrian journeys across the city.

We aim to provide Hong Kong's Transport Complaints Unit with thousands of data points per quarter, boosting institutional capacity to tackle some of the worst blackspots and danger zones we face every day.

This app is a civic engagement and data crowdsourcing tool from Transit Jam, Hong Kong's trusted road safety advocacy team.

🌐 International Safe System Alignment
This application is built in complete accordance with the World Bank Global Road Safety Facility (GRSF) 2026 Management Guidelines, addressing critical data gaps and converting crowdsourced observations into standardised geographic datasets for automated institutional parsing.

📋 Frequently Asked Questions

1. Will this lead to the immediate prosecution of drivers?

No. This utility is strictly an objective data collection tool built to support the identification of systemic urban infrastructure issues and parking blackspots. It compiles raw location statistics to help authorities analyze macro traffic behavior, rather than functioning as an active tool for immediate criminal prosecution.

2. Is my privacy secure when using this app?

Absolutely. ZAP! is engineered as a purely static web application with no databases, cookies, backend tracking scripts, or analytics modules. The tool accesses your device's GPS coordinates locally inside your browser window for a fraction of a second purely to format the text draft, immediately forgetting it the moment your email client opens. Your data never touches an external server.

3. Why does the app launch my phone's native email client?

By launching your native mail app, ZAP! ensures you retain 100% executive control over the report. You can see exactly what text is being sent, add specific descriptions or context if desired, and send it securely through your own email address. This transparent setup guarantees no hidden data is being transmitted behind your back.

4. Does ZAP! consume a significant amount of mobile data?

No, the data usage is practically zero. Loading the entire application over a mobile connection takes less than 7 Kilobytes (KB), about a thousandth of an online newspaper page; sending the email report uses less than 1 KB. The web-app is explicitly engineered to be ultra-lightweight, lightning-fast, and function seamlessly even in remote areas.

5. I am a commercial or private driver - how does parking reform benefit me?

Measuring illegal parking and other road hazards will help planners identify blackspots and find solutions. Researchers the world over have found proper management (and pricing) of the curb reduces cruising and congestion, and makes driving easier, faster (on average), and smoother for everyone.
