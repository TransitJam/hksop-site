Hong Kong School of P arking

Illegal parking and pavement obstruction degrade our public spaces, create severe safety hazards for pedestrians, and trap those using wheelchairs or pushing strollers.

Traditional methods of reporting can feel cumbersome, requiring multiple steps, app downloads, or long forms. This utility was born out of a desire to create a simple, frictionless, "one-button reporting" alternative. By automatically capturing your location and timestamp, it allows citizens to log incidents securely in seconds.

📢 Trust & Community:
This initiative is proudly aligned with the values of walkability and road transparency. By operating completely transparently via open-source code and functioning as a fully static web page, we guarantee that your personal data never touches external servers or tracking databases.
